Built for creative independence.

Our plan is to build the product and infrastructure in stages, use open-source tools where sensible, and develop our own music model rather than quietly forwarding prompts to a third-party music API.

🎼

Our music model

Research, data preparation, model experiments and evaluation toward original text-to-music generation.

🧰

Our infrastructure

Build portable backend, database, storage and job-processing components with minimal vendor lock-in.

🎧

Creator experience

Design for prompts, musical styles, saved tracks, playback and future audio editing tools.

ℹ️
Honest status

This is the first frontend milestone. No song is generated yet; real generation will only be enabled after a model and compute environment are connected and tested.

One milestone at a time.

We will develop from your Android phone through GitHub, starting with the foundation before attempting resource-intensive model training.

Phase 01

Foundation

Responsive website, documented architecture, repository structure, security rules and local development workflow.

Phase 02

Own services

Backend API, database schema, audio storage abstraction, generation queue and health checks.

Phase 03

Model research

Choose a training approach, validate dataset rights, run small experiments and measure audio quality before scaling.
